## Drop 1 (bars 33-56, 1:00.000-1:45.000)

- Continuous 24 bars, no drop-outs.
- **Kick** every beat. **Sub** note on the 16th after each kick (sidechained rolling bass). **Pad** pumps on every beat.
- **Clap** on 2 and 4, lighter percussive layer on every beat. **Open hat** on every offbeat, top loop in 16ths.
- **Bass line** inside each 4-bar loop: Ab→G | F→Ab | **C** | Bb→A. The **Cm bar (bars 35, 39, 43, 47, 51, 55)** is ~3 dB heavier: that's where the deep camera pushes and the biggest images go.
- **Vocal chops:** 1:07.7, 1:09.2-1:10.8, 1:24.6, 1:28.0-1:31.3.
- **Phrase lines:** every 4 bars (1:07.5, 1:15.0, 1:22.5, 1:30.0, 1:37.5). Second half of the drop starts at 1:30.0 (bar 49).
- **Lift:** bars 55-56 (1:41.25-1:45.0), more mids and highs, "Moving to the music" returns at 1:40.93.
- **End:** last kick 1:45.000, the floor disappears into the breakdown.

## Build 2 → Drop 2

Same shape as build 1, 105 s later: claps from 2:30.0, "Out of sight" 2:31.59, "Take a chance" 2:33.73, **kick out 2:35.625**, 8ths 2:37.5, 16ths 2:41.25, "Moving to the music" 2:40.96, "grooving till the bar kicks us out" 2:42.95, breath 2:44.53, **DROP 2 at 2:45.000** on "OUT".

## Drop 2 (bars 89-112, 2:45.000-3:30.000)

- Same groove as drop 1 for the first 15 bars.
- **Vocal chop** 3:07.6.
- **Bar 104 (3:13.125-3:15.000): the kick drops out for a whole bar**, claps keep going. This is the only internal drop-out in either drop.
- **3:15.000: kick slams back.** The second-half escalation of the whole song.
- **Bar 112 (3:28.125-3:30.000): kick out again** under a final vocal phrase (3:26.2-3:31.6).
- **3:30.000:** final hit, outro.
