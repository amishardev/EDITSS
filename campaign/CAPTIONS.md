# Dietary Guide posting plan

Captions and an upload schedule for all 20 films, 20 Oct to 9 Nov 2026. Every caption is ready to copy and paste as-is, hashtags included.

- All times are IST. Post as Reels and turn on "Also share to feed". Cross-post the same file to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels if you use them.
- Pin the Dussehra app film and The Wellness Treat to the top of the grid while the festive season runs.
- Stories: reshare each Reel to Stories about 2 hours after posting. From 4 to 7 Nov, reshare The Wellness Treat with a link sticker to dietaryguide.in/products.
- 8 Nov is Diwali and the slot is left open. There's no Diwali film yet, so ask for one and it'll go there.
- Every claim in the captions comes from the pack or the app itself. Prices, offers and delivery promises are left out on purpose.
