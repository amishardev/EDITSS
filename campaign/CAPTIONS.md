# Dietary Guide posting plan

Captions and an upload schedule for all 50 posts (20 promo films and 30 Food Facts), 20 Oct to 23 Nov 2026. Every caption is ready to copy and paste as-is, hashtags included.

- All times are IST. Post as Reels and turn on "Also share to feed". Cross-post the same file to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels if you use them.
- Two series overlap until 9 Nov: the promo films and the daily Food Fact. On days with both, they are at least 4 hours apart.
- Post the Food Facts in this order. The "Food Fact No." inside each video matches its day, from No.01 on 25 Oct to No.30 on 23 Nov.
- Pin the Dussehra app film and The Wellness Treat to the top of the grid while the festive season runs.
- Stories: reshare each Reel to Stories about 2 hours after posting. From 4 to 7 Nov, reshare The Wellness Treat with a link sticker to dietaryguide.in/products.
- 8 Nov is Diwali: Food Fact No.15 goes out in the morning, and the promo slot is still open because there's no Diwali film yet.
- Every claim comes from the pack, the app, or the source named at the end of each Food Fact caption. No prices, offers or medical promises.

`out/edu/edu-05-intermittent-fasting.mp4` · Food Fact No.05 · Midday, when the fasting window crowd breaks their fast.

Intermittent fasting sab kar rahe hain. Par kya ye sach mein jaadu hai? ⏰

NEJM mein chhapi 12 mahine ki ek study mein dono groups ne calories kam ki. Ek group ne sirf 8 ghante ki window mein khaaya. Weight loss: 8.0 kg vs 6.3 kg, aur farak statistically significant nahi tha.
