## Before uploading (important for reach)

1. **Content ID:** if MIZRAB went out through a distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, etc.) with YouTube Content ID on, whitelist your own channel there first, or every Short can get claimed.
2. **Related video (this replaces links):** on each Short, set "Related video" to the full MIZRAB upload. It shows as a tappable button on the Short. Needs the full song on your channel as a normal video (or a YT Music release).
3. **Instagram:** put the Spotify/YouTube link in your bio (or a link-in-bio page). Captions just say "link in bio".
4. **Same title format, different words:** don't copy-paste titles. They're already all different above.
5. **Watch the first 3 days:** if a Short gets under ~60% "viewed vs swiped away" (Studio > Content > Shorts), push the next one back a day and post something unrelated in between.
