# REFERENCE ONLY

Not source footage. Nothing from these titles is in this project and nothing from them should be cut into a Reel: they're copyrighted films. They're listed because their night language is close to what MOONLIGHT should feel like, and they're useful to point an editor or collaborator at.

| Film | What to borrow (as an idea, not an image) | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| *Fallen Angels* (Wong Kar-wai, 1995) | the closing motorcycle ride through a tunnel: speed, sodium light, a moment of peace inside motion. The tunnel-exit Reels come from this feeling | the step-printed smear look, the characters |
| *Chungking Express* (Wong Kar-wai, 1994) | the city as a blur of other people's lives; one person still while everything moves | specific shots |
| *Before Sunrise* (Richard Linklater, 1995) | a whole night of walking and honest talk that ends at dawn: the truth lines and the afterglow Reels | dialogue-driven scenes |
| *Night on Earth* (Jim Jarmusch, 1991) | one night, many rides, the taxi window as a frame | city-specific imagery |
| *Collateral* (Michael Mann, 2004) | digital night city with sodium and haze, the sky never fully black | the thriller tone |
| *Drive* (Nicolas Winding Refn, 2011) | night driving as rhythm; restraint between bursts | the violence, the jacket, the font clichés it spawned |
| *Aftersun* (Charlotte Wells, 2022) | dancing remembered through fragments and flashes; bittersweet memory of a night | strobe-heavy imagery |

Rule of thumb: if a frame could be recognised as "that scene from that film", it's too close.
